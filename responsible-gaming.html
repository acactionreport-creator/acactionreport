Responsible Gaming

AC Action Report supports responsible gaming and believes gambling should be treated as entertainment, not a source of income or financial recovery.

Never gamble with money you cannot afford to lose. Set personal limits for time and spending before participating in any casino or gaming activity.

If gambling stops being enjoyable or begins negatively impacting finances, relationships, work, or mental health, seek help immediately.
